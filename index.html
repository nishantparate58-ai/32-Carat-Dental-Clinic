Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have answers.

Is a root canal painful?

Not at all! With modern anesthesia and our advanced techniques, a root canal is virtually painless and feels similar to getting a routine filling.

How long does teeth whitening last?

Teeth whitening can last anywhere from 6 months to 2 years, depending on your dietary habits (like drinking coffee or tea) and oral hygiene routine.

Do you offer emergency services?

Yes, we prioritize dental emergencies. Please call our clinic directly at +91 9545 20 10 25 for immediate assistance.

What is the cost of braces?

The cost varies depending on the type of braces (metal, ceramic, or invisible aligners) and the complexity of your case. We offer a free initial consultation to provide an exact estimate.
